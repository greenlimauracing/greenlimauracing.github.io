About the Driver

Biography

Luqman Fazrul is a Malaysian motorsport competitor representing Green Limau Racing in Simracing while participating as an Academy Karter for Yung Motorsport. Through both disciplines, he continues to develop his racecraft, consistency, and competitive mindset while gaining valuable experience on and off the track. Committed to continuous improvement, Luqman approaches every event with professionalism and determination as he works towards his long-term goal of progressing into GT racing.
