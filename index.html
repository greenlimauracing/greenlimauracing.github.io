About GLR

Green Limau Racing is a Malaysian motorsport organisation founded in 2025. Based in Selangor, Malaysia, GLR focuses on developing drivers through sim racing, karting and professional driver development. Our mission is to create a pathway for talented drivers to progress from grassroots motorsport into real-world racing.

GLR Academy

The GLR Academy is designed to develop the next generation of racing drivers through structured training, race preparation and professional motorsport knowledge. Drivers progress through a pathway focused on consistency, racecraft and discipline.



01

Driving Fundamentals

Understanding racing lines, braking points, car control and consistency.

02

Racecraft Development

Improving overtaking, defending, strategy and race awareness.

03

Professional Preparation

Building driver discipline, communication and competition mindset.



Driver Pathway

Certification
