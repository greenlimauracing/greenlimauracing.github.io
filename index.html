About GLR

Green Limau Racing is a Malaysian motorsport organisation founded in 2025. Based in Selangor, Malaysia, GLR focuses on developing drivers through sim racing, karting and professional driver development. Our mission is to create a pathway for talented drivers to progress from grassroots motorsport into real-world racing.
